Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before starting your African adventure with URUMURI.

How can I book a tour with URUMURI?

You can send us a booking request through our website or contact our team directly on WhatsApp. We will get back to you to discuss your travel plans.

Which destinations do you offer?

We offer travel experiences in Rwanda, Kenya, Uganda and Tanzania, with other destinations available upon request.

Can I customize my travel package?

Yes. We can help you create a personalized travel experience based on your interests, travel dates, group size and budget.

Does a booking request confirm my reservation?

No. Sending a booking request does not automatically confirm your reservation. Our team will contact you to discuss availability and the next steps.

Do you organize family and group tours?

Yes. We can arrange travel experiences for individuals, couples, families and groups.

How can I contact your team?

You can reach us through our contact section or WhatsApp. We will be happy to assist with your travel inquiries.
